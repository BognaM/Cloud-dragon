About talking to an AI dragon

Your cloud dragon is an AI, not a person or a therapist. It runs on a small language model, so it can misunderstand you, make things up, or give advice that doesn't fit your situation. Double-check anything important.

It can help with

  • putting your thoughts into words, or journaling prompts
  • a pep talk, or planning a calmer day or evening
  • simple ideas for rest, focus and small routines

It can't replace

  • a doctor, therapist or counselor
  • diagnosis, or advice about medication
  • support in a crisis

If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out to a person now. If you can, also talk to someone you trust. In Switzerland:

  • 144 medical emergency, day and night
  • 143 Die Dargebotene Hand · La Main Tendue · Telefono Amico: someone to talk to, day and night, free and anonymous. Chat and email at 143.ch
  • 0800 143 000 the same in English, 6–11 pm
  • 147 Pro Juventute, for children and young people, day and night, free. Also WhatsApp, at 147.ch

Someone hurting or threatening you? 142 victim support, free and day and night, or 117 police if you're in danger now.

Alcohol, drugs or medication? 145 for poisoning and questions about mixing, day and night. Anonymous online advice at safezone.ch.

Worried about eating or your body? Free advice from 043 488 63 73 (AES, in German) or 021 329 04 39 (ABA, in French).

Psychiatric emergency services in each canton: list by canton (PDF, UPD Bern).

Outside Switzerland? Find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

Your privacy

Your messages go to a cloud server to create the replies. The server doesn't keep a chat history and doesn't write your messages to its logs. Your conversation and bond are saved only in this browser. Avoid sharing details you'd want to keep fully private.
